## Stage 4 evidence: what `cancel()` actually does

Six direct-engine trials ran (`IMovementEngine.goTo` 40 blocks, then `cancel()` after k ticks), plus the contract trial (S3). The columns are ticks after `cancel()` until each signal was last active, within a 200-tick window.

| Cancel after | segment | next | calc | goal | inputs | process | physical stop | moved after cancel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| k=0 (same tick) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 blocks |
| k=1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.00 |
| k=5 | 112 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 111 | 0 | 118 | **38.41** |
| k=20 | 98 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 97 | 0 | 104 | 34.36 |
| k=40 | 78 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 77 | 0 | 84 | 27.64 |
| k=60 | 58 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 57 | 0 | 64 | 20.41 |
| S3, contract (k=30, 24 blocks) | 41 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 40 | 0 | 47 | 14.04 |

### Findings (Verified by this run; flat world, Baritone backend)

- **L1. `cancel()` does not stop an executing path segment.** Once a segment is executing, the player keeps walking and finishes the segment at the original target. In every trial with k≥5 the player ended at the target. Cancel only took effect before the first segment was installed (k=0, k=1). This is existing Ostinato behaviour; direct `IMovementEngine` use behaves the same.
- **L2. Four of the six raw signals are not evidence of release.** `CONTROLLING_PROCESS`, `TRAVEL_GOAL_ACTIVE`, `PATH_CALCULATION_PENDING` and `NEXT_SEGMENT_PRESENT` all went quiet **at the moment of cancel** while the engine kept steering for up to 112 more ticks. A rule built on them would declare the resource safe while it is still in use.
- **L3. `PATH_SEGMENT_PRESENT` and `FORCED_INPUTS` tracked real control.** Forced inputs cleared exactly one tick before the segment cleared, in all seven cancels. Forced inputs were never observed without a segment.
- **L4. Physical stop lags control release by about 6 ticks.** That is momentum decay after the keys are released, not engine control.
- **L5. No delayed-result re-arm (race R1) was observed** in these trials: no segment re-appeared after clearing. This does not show R1 cannot happen.

### Cause of L1

**Unknown.** Ostinato's `PathExecutor.onTick()` reports "safe to cancel" only "if a movement just finished", and its execution includes look-ahead drivers (`DRIVER_PATIENCE`). On this flat path no safe point was reported mid-segment, so `PathingControlManager`'s per-tick `cancelSegmentIfSafe()` never fired before the segment ended (stage1-audit §3.2). Confirming the exact mechanism needs a trace of `PathExecutor` / `Movement.safeToCancel()` on this path.

## Consequences

- **Conductor's Stage 3 rules held up.** Reporting `CANCELLING` rather than `CANCELLED`, and refusing a second grant, were correct. A contract that reported "cancelled" after `cancel()` would have been wrong for up to about 5.6 seconds in this run.
- **Candidate Stage 4 release evidence now has live support.** The candidate is `PATH_SEGMENT_PRESENT=false`, `FORCED_INPUTS` empty and `PATH_CALCULATION_PENDING=false`, sustained, then a short momentum allowance before reuse. It matched real control release in 7 of 7 cancels on flat ground with the Baritone backend. It is still unvalidated for:
  - terrain with parkour, pillaring or water;
  - elytra;
  - Tungsten;
  - concurrent processes such as mining;
  - `forceCancel()` callers (R1).
- **Cancel latency is bounded by the remaining segment, not by Conductor.** Making cancel faster would need a different engine stop. Ostinato already has one, `IPathingBehavior.forceCancel()`, but using it changes stop semantics and invalidates the in-progress-calculation signal (R1), so it is a decision for the project owner, not something Conductor should switch to silently.

\* Our segment cleared at the water's edge. The later activity is AirProcess (drowning protection), which the analyzer reports separately.

- **L1 extended.** On staircase, trench and long paths, cancel let the executing segment finish, for up to 344 ticks (17 s) and 122 blocks. Water was the exception: the first safe point was the water's edge.
- **Timing boundary.** A cancel 1 tick after `goTo` came before the first segment existed (3/3 no movement). From 2 ticks on, the segment had started and ran to completion (9/9).
- **L3 held in all 24 trials.** Movement more than 10 ticks after segment and inputs cleared: 0. Momentum took 6–11 ticks.
- **L5.** No re-arm in 24 trials.
- **New: foreign control after release.** AirProcess took control after the operation was released. Stage 4 classifies this as DETECTED and re-quarantines.
- **B6 live.** Navigation `cancel()` stopped a running `MineProcess` immediately.
